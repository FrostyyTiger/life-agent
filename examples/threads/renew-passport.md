Expires 4 November. The appointment slots at the passport office run about four weeks out,
and the form needs a countersignature plus photos before that — hence the six week lead.

The failure mode this exists to prevent: knowing perfectly well the passport expires in
November, and discovering in mid-October that the next available slot is in December.
